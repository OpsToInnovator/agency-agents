## Who runs the beta

The SkillCurrent beta is run by ApexForm Life Pty Ltd (ABN 11 698 784 904),
of Perth, Western Australia (apexformlife.com). "We" below means ApexForm
Life. Write to us at now@apexformlife.com.

## What the beta is

- A free pilot of SkillCurrent for five to ten teams, for four to six weeks
  from your install session.
- You run the software on your own machines. We help you set it up and
  answer questions on your email thread, aiming to reply within two working
  days (Perth time).
- There is nothing to sign. Replying "I agree" to the email that sent you
  these terms is enough. The person who replies should be able to agree for
  their team.

## What we collect, and why

| What | Where it comes from | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Your sign-up: work email, team size, tools, note, the link that brought you | an email your own email app sends from the beta page | to reply and to choose pilot teams |
| Weekly beta reports: counts only, no skill content, names or emails | you send them by email | to answer the beta's two questions |
| What you tell us on calls and by email, and our notes of it | you | to support you and decide what to build next |

The beta page doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking, and its form
doesn't send anything to a server. The page's host keeps standard access
logs, which include your network address, for its own security and
operation. We don't receive or use those logs.

## How we use it

- We use it to support you during the beta and to decide what to build.
- We will not publish your name, your company's name, your logo, a quote,
  or any of your numbers without your written permission.
- We may describe results across all pilot teams together, such as "four
  of seven teams kept review switched on", in a way that does not identify
  any team.
- We don't sell what you give us, and we don't add you to a marketing list.
  Beta emails are about the beta; if we ever write about anything else, you
  can opt out by replying.

## Who processes it for us

- **Email:** Apple (iCloud Mail on the apexformlife.com domain) stores our
  email.
- **Beta page hosting:** GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages) hosts the beta page.
- **Calls:** if we meet by video, we use the call service we agree with you
  for that call.

These providers may store or process information outside Australia,
including in the United States. We choose providers we consider reputable
and use them only to run the beta.

## How long we keep it

We keep sign-ups, reports and notes until six months after the beta ends,
then delete them. Ask at any time and we will delete yours sooner, and
confirm when it's done. You can also ask to see or correct what we hold
about you.

## Confidentiality

We treat what you tell us about your team and your work as confidential.
You're free to say whatever you like about SkillCurrent.

## Feedback

Feedback is optional. If you give it, we may use it to improve SkillCurrent
without owing you anything for it. You're under no obligation to buy
anything, now or later.

## Ending the beta

Either of us can stop at any time, for any reason, by email. `BETA.md`
explains how to remove everything from your machines.

After the beta, SkillCurrent's source stays available under its open-source
licence, and nothing you use during the beta will stop working. If we
introduce a paid plan, pilot teams will be told first and get it free for
12 months after it launches. You are under no obligation to take it.

## Questions and complaints

Write to now@apexformlife.com. We'll reply within two working days and aim
to resolve any concern within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with how we
handled a privacy concern, you can contact the Office of the Australian
Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

## Changes

If we change these terms during the beta, we'll email you the new version
and what changed. If you don't agree, you can stop.
